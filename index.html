01 About

A quiet standard in a rarified world — we place companions, never commodities.

Deore & Freond was founded on a simple conviction: that the bond between a family and its companion deserves the same care, rigour and discretion as any other enduring commitment. We work quietly, with a small number of families each year.

Our network of breeders, veterinarians and logistics partners spans four continents. Every introduction is deliberate, every lineage documented, and every journey arranged so that nothing is left to chance.

02 Philosophy

More than a dog. A lifelong bond. We believe the right bond is built on purpose, not chance.

Our role is to connect discerning families with extraordinary bloodlines through integrity, discretion and unwavering care. Every placement begins with listening — to your home, your rhythms, your ambitions for the life you want to share.
